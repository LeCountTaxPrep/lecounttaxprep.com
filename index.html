Meet the Owner
Meet the Owner

Hi, I’m Chelsea LeCount.

I began my career in public accounting and have spent years working across tax, bookkeeping, payroll, audits, and small-business accounting. Today, I bring that technical experience together with the part of business I enjoy most: creating structure and building systems that make things work better.

I genuinely love taking something that feels scattered, inefficient, or overwhelming and turning it into a process that makes sense. My work goes beyond entering transactions. I help business owners clean up what is not working, strengthen the financial back office, and create reliable systems they can actually use as they grow.

Outside of work, I’m a mom, a beekeeper, and a naturally creative person. Beekeeping appeals to me for many of the same reasons accounting does: healthy systems depend on structure, consistency, attention to detail, and knowing how all the moving pieces work together. I also enjoy building new ideas, working on creative projects, and finding better ways to organize just about anything. I’m continuing my professional education and working toward CPA licensure.

LeCount Tax Prep was built around direct, hands-on support. Clients work with me personally, with an emphasis on accuracy, organization, practical problem-solving, and solutions that fit the way their business actually operates.

Chelsea LeCount • Owner, LeCount Tax Prep
